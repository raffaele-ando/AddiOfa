
=================== Prepositions of Time ===================
[q9] [B1|Traduzione]
  P: Translate 'Il treno partirà tra 2 minuti.'
  O: ["The train leaves between 2 minutes.","The train will leave in 2 minutes.","The train going to leave on 2 minutes.","The train part in 2 minutes."]
  C: The train will leave in 2 minutes.
  E: We use 'in' for future time limits ('in 2 minutes'). 'Will leave' expresses a future fact.
[q10] [B1|Traduzione]
  P: Translate 'Ti posso offrire qualcosa da bere?'
  O: ["Can I offer you anything for drink?","Can I offer you something to drink?","Can I offer you somewhat to drink?","Do I can offer you something to drink?"]
  C: Can I offer you something to drink?
  E: In offers, we normally use 'something', not 'anything'. The infinitive is 'to drink'.
[q11] [B1|Traduzione]
  P: Translate 'Francesco lavora per Google.'
  O: ["Francesco work to Google.","Francesco works for Google.","Francesco is working by Google.","Francesco working for Google."]
  C: Francesco works for Google.
  E: Third person singular in Present Simple requires an 's' ('works'). 'Work for' is the correct preposition.
[q15] [B1|Traduzione]
  P: Translate 'Francesco riesce a parlare bene inglese.'
  O: ["Francesco can to speak English well.","Francesco can speaks English well.","Francesco is able speak English good.","Francesco can speak English well."]
  C: Francesco can speak English well.
  E: Modal verb 'can' is followed by the base form without 'to' ('can speak').
[q23] [B1|Grammatica]
  P: Complete the sentence: 'What time _____ your plane take off?'
  O: ["do","is","does","has"]
  C: does
  E: For scheduled future events, we use Present Simple. 'Plane' is 'it', so we use 'does'.
[q24] [B1|Grammatica]
  P: Which question is correct?
  O: ["Can I offer you anything to drink?","Can I to offer you anything to drink?","Do I can offer you anything to drink?","Can I offer you anything for drinking?"]
  C: Can I offer you anything to drink?
  E: Modal 'can' is followed by a bare infinitive ('offer'). The object complement is a full infinitive ('to drink').
[q26] [B1|Grammatica]
  P: Complete the sentence: 'James can speak English very _____.'
  O: ["good","better","best","well"]
  C: well
  E: 'Well' is the adverb form of 'good' and modifies the verb 'speak'.
[q30] [B1|Grammatica]
  P: Complete the sentence: 'What time does the train leave tomorrow? It _____ at 9.'
  O: ["leave","is leaving","leaving","leaves"]
  C: leaves
  E: For a scheduled timetable in the future, we use the Present Simple ('leaves').
